# Core Problem: The Payment Operations Visibility Gap

## Context
In modern online commerce and fintech ecosystems, payment conversion is the lifeblood of business revenue. However, payment failures are often treated in isolation. Merchants can view individual payment logs, but when a systemic incident occurs, identifying the underlying root cause is slow, manual, and expensive.

## The 9 Critical Questions Every Merchant Faces
When payment performance suddenly degrades, a merchant operations manager must rapidly answer:
1. **What changed?** (e.g. Success rate dropped by 28 percentage points)
2. **When did it start?** (e.g. Exactly at 14:02 UTC)
3. **Which payment methods are affected?** (e.g. UPI vs Cards vs Net Banking)
4. **Which banks, device types, or locations are concentrated?** (e.g. HDFC Bank UPI on Android in Mumbai)
5. **What are the most likely causes?** (e.g. External bank timeout vs merchant gateway config vs customer OTP drop-off)
6. **How many transactions and customers are affected?**
7. **What is the estimated business / revenue exposure?**
8. **What safe, bounded action should be taken right now?**
9. **Did the action actually improve the situation?** (Closed-loop verification)
